Paired devices

Devices that have paired with Pocket Nova (all slots). Give them names so you know which is which. Removing one makes that device forget Pocket Nova, so it has to pair again.

Connect pop-up on Windows

While Pocket Nova is ready to pair, a nearby Windows PC shows "New Bluetooth device found, Connect". One click pairs it. This is Microsoft's Swift Pair. It's on when nothing is paired, and for 3 minutes after Pocket Nova starts or switches slot. On the PC it also needs Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Devices → "Show notifications to connect using Swift Pair".

How to pair a new device

On Windows: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth → choose Pocket Nova. On a phone, open Bluetooth settings and tap it. If it doesn't show up, the slot is still connected to another device: switch slot or forget that device first.

Your TV

Power works on any TV without picking anything. It sends every TV's power code (your TV's first) over about 15 seconds. Tap the Atom once your TV switches off to stop it. Volume, mute, channels and input need your TV's brand: pick it here, or let Pocket Nova find it.

Find my TV

Point Pocket Nova at the TV and press start. It tries many power codes, one every 1.3 seconds, with the lights filling up as it goes. Tap the Atom the moment your TV switches off. It then switches the TV back on to double-check: tap again to save. Hold the Atom to cancel.

Test buttons

Point the Atom at the TV, within about 2 metres, then press a button here. On the Atom you can also tilt forward or back and hold to keep changing the volume.

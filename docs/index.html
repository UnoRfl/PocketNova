Pocket Nova

A pocket remote, clicker and pet on a 5×5 LED screen, with a control panel on your PC. One button and a tilt sensor run everything. This guide covers every control, every app, Nova the pet, the PC panel and the phone remote.

Download and install

You need an M5Stack Atom Matrix and a USB-C cable that carries data (some only charge).

Download the latest version
  1. Firmware: download PocketNova-firmware.bin. Plug the Atom into a computer, open Espressif's web flasher in Chrome or Edge, click Connect and pick the Atom's port. Set the flash address to 0x0, choose the file and click Program.
  2. PC panel (Windows, optional): download PocketNova-Panel.zip, unzip it, right-click install.ps1 and choose "Run with PowerShell". It needs Python 3. The panel then opens by itself whenever Pocket Nova is plugged in.
  3. Phone remote: nothing to install. Open the remote page in Chrome on Android.

The three moves

The whole top face of the Atom is the button.

HoldAbout 1 second. Opens the menu from the pet, and goes back one step everywhere else.
TapA quick press. Opens the app you're on, or does the thing the screen shows.
TiltTip it left or right about 37° to move one item. Bring it back flat before the next one.
Double tapOn Nova: jump straight back into the last app you used. In Media: mute.

The first time Pocket Nova starts, a short tutorial teaches these moves. You can replay it from Settings or the PC panel.

Pet (home)— hold → Menu— tap → App— hold → Menu— hold → Pet

How tilting works

  • One item per tilt. Holding a tilt does not keep scrolling. Return close to flat, then tilt again.
  • Watch the edge glow. A faint light grows on the side you're tilting toward. When it's bright, the next item is about to slide in.
  • Whatever angle you're holding it at counts as flat. The Atom resets "level" each time you open an app, so you don't need to hold it perfectly flat.
  • Bumps are ignored. A quick knock, or the wobble from pressing the button, won't move anything.
  • Rest on an icon for a moment and its name scrolls across the screen.

Nova, the pet

Nova lives on the home screen. Nova reacts to almost everything you do, but only a hold takes you into the menu, and a double tap opens your last app.

  • Rocking means tilting it gently left and right a few times, like a cradle.
  • Face down makes Nova hide and the screen go dark. Turn it back over and Nova pops out.
  • Coming back from an app: Nova is happy if what you did worked, and sad (with a tear) if something failed.
  • Left alone: Nova gets bored after 45 seconds and looks around and yawns. After 2 minutes Nova falls asleep.
  • At night (10 pm to 7 am): Nova's eyes are sleepy and Nova dozes off after 30 seconds. The time comes from the PC panel.
  • Affection goes up by 1 for each tap and by 3 for a triple tap. From 50, a little heart floats up now and then. The PC panel shows the score.

The menu goes back to Nova by itself after 25 seconds of no use.

Power works on any TV. Tapping Power sends the power code of every TV Pocket Nova knows (your picked TV first), over about 15 seconds, with the lights filling up as it goes. Tap again once your TV switches off to stop, so it doesn’t get switched back on by a later code. Every other button (volume, mute, channels, input) needs your TV picked first: tap the brand button (it shows a blinking ? until you pick one) or use Find TV. Until then those buttons look dim and show “pick your TV”.

Find TV picks your TV for you: it tries power codes slowly, one every 1.3 seconds. Tap the moment your TV switches off, Pocket Nova switches it back on to double-check, and you tap again to save. Volume dial: once your TV is picked, tilt forward or back and hold to keep turning the volume up or down.

Sensor

Level

A spirit level. The dot drifts to the high side. It's green when level (within about 2°), yellow when close and red when far off. Tap to set the current angle as level, for example when your desk isn't flat.

Fun

Dice

Shake it or tap to roll. The face tumbles and slows down before it lands.

Tool

Timer

  • Tilt to choose the length: 1, 3, 5, 10, 15, 25, 45 or 60 minutes. Two-digit numbers scroll.
  • Tap to start. The 25 lights drain as time runs out, changing from green to red.
  • Tap to pause (the lights blink), and tap again to carry on.
  • It flashes rainbow when the time is up. Tap to set a new time.

Leaving the app with a hold stops the timer.

Ambient

Lights

Mood lighting. Tap or tilt to change the effect: rainbow, fire, ocean, breathe, tilt (the colour follows the way you tip it) and sparkle.

Tool

Torch

A flashlight. Tap to switch between white, warm, red (keeps your night vision) and off. It turns itself off after 3 minutes so the LEDs don't get hot.

Game

Snake

Tap to start, then tilt to steer. Eat the blinking red dots. You can pass through the edges and come out the other side, but don't run into yourself. The snake speeds up as it grows. Your score and best score scroll at the end, with "NEW BEST" when you beat it. Tap to play again.

The PC panel

The Pocket Nova Panel is a program on your PC that gives you every setting with normal buttons. It runs quietly in the background and opens by itself the moment you plug Pocket Nova in. You can also open it from the Start menu.

Bluetooth slots: each slot is a separate Bluetooth identity, so your devices don't fight over Pocket Nova. Pair your PC on slot 1 and your phone on slot 2, then switch slots in the panel (or Settings → Bluetooth, double tap) to change which one it controls.

Factory reset erases every setting, forgets every paired device and resets Nova's affection. Pocket Nova then starts again with the tutorial. In the panel, type RESET to unlock the button.

The panel only listens to this PC. Other websites can't control Pocket Nova through it.

Wi-Fi setup

Wi-Fi lets Pocket Nova get the time from the internet, so Nova knows when it's night even on a phone charger. It's off until you set it up.

  1. On the Atom, go to Settings → Wi-Fi and double tap. Or use the PC panel: Wi-Fi → Open setup network.
  2. The icon pulses magenta, and the network name (NOVA- plus 4 characters) and an 8-digit password scroll past. Tap the icon to see them again.
  3. On your phone, join that network with that password. A setup page opens by itself. If it doesn't, open http://192.168.4.1.
  4. Pick your home network, type its password and tap Connect. The page shows "Connected!" and the setup network closes.

The icon is green when online, yellow while joining and red if joining failed (tap it to see why). The setup network closes by itself after 10 minutes, and each time it opens it has a new password.

Phone remote

The remote page controls Pocket Nova from your phone over Bluetooth: the buttons, tilting, the TV remote, Nova and brightness, with a live copy of the screen. Your PC stays connected as the keyboard at the same time.

  1. Open the remote page in Chrome on Android (or Chrome/Edge on a computer). On an iPhone, use the free Bluefy browser, because Safari can't use Bluetooth from a web page.
  2. Tap Connect to Pocket Nova and pick it from the list.
  3. A 4-digit code scrolls on Pocket Nova's lights. Type it in. This proves you're next to the device, so nobody nearby can take over.

The phone can't forget pairings, rename the device or factory reset it. Those stay on the device and the PC panel. The remote also sets Pocket Nova's clock from your phone.

First-time setup

Do this once if the icons look sideways or tilting goes the wrong way. The PC panel's Device tab can also swap left/right or forward/back with one click.

  1. Hold on the pet to open the menu, then tilt until you reach Settings (the gear). Tap.
  2. Tilt to Rotate. Tap until the arrow points away from you.
  3. Tilt to Calibrate and tap. A blinking arrow appears. Tip the Atom toward the arrow and tap.
  4. A second arrow appears. Tip toward that one and tap. "OK" scrolls when it's saved.

Always do Rotate before Calibrate. If Calibrate shows a red X, you didn't tilt far enough or tilted the same way twice. Try again.

If something's wrong

The Bluetooth apps show a pulsing yellow sign

Pocket Nova isn't connected. On Windows, a "New Bluetooth device found, Connect" pop-up appears while Pocket Nova is ready to pair (when nothing is paired, or for 3 minutes after it starts or switches slot): click Connect. Or open Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth, then choose Pocket Nova. Once you've paired it, it reconnects by itself every time. If it doesn't appear, the current slot is still paired to another device. Switch slot, or remove that device in the panel's Bluetooth tab.

The TV doesn't react
  • Check the brand letter in the TV app, or use Find TV.
  • Get closer (within 2 metres) and point the Atom straight at the TV.
  • Try a different angle. The IR light only shines one way.
Tilting moves the wrong way or the pictures are sideways

Follow the first-time setup above: Rotate, then Calibrate.

The PC doesn't see the Atom when it's plugged in

Use a USB-C cable that carries data. Cables that come with microphones often only charge. A working connection shows up as COM3.

The panel didn't open when I plugged it in

Open it from the Start menu: Pocket Nova Panel. It only opens itself once per plug-in, and not if it's already open. To reinstall it, run install.ps1 from the panel download again.

Reinstalling the firmware

The easiest way is the web flasher from Download and install. To build it yourself, get the source code and, with the Atom plugged in, run this from the firmware folder (ESP32 core 2.0.17, FastLED 3.6.0, M5Atom, IRremoteESP8266 and ArduinoJson 7 installed):

arduino-cli compile --fqbn "esp32:esp32:m5stack-atom:PartitionScheme=min_spiffs" PocketNova
arduino-cli upload -p COM3 --fqbn "esp32:esp32:m5stack-atom:PartitionScheme=min_spiffs,UploadSpeed=115200" PocketNova

An upload takes about 80 seconds. Don't unplug it partway through, or it will restart over and over until you upload again.

Make it yours

Every picture in this guide is drawn from the same text the Atom uses. Open Sprites.h and each icon is 5 rows of 5 letters, where a letter is a colour (R red, G green, B blue, Y yellow, W white) and a dot is off. Change the letters, re-upload, and your icon is on the device.
